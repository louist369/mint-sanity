mint-sanity

Paste a Solana token mint. This page reads DexScreener’s public pair data and prints a few numbers — not a verdict.

Why this exists: most “is this mint real?” checks dump you into a chart, a wallet connect, or a thread of screenshots. This is a one-field lookup so you can see a name, a symbol, and whether any pool has reported liquidity, then close the tab. It is not research, not a score, and not advice to buy, sell, or hold.
